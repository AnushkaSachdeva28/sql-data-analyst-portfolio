# HR Attrition Analysis
---
## Business Question
Which employees are most likely to leave the company, and what factors are associated with leaving?
---
## Dataset
---
- **Source:** IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition dataset
- **Records:** 1,470 employees
- **Purpose:** Analyze employee attrition and workforce trends.
---
## Tools and SQL Concepts

- **Tool:** MySQL
- **SQL Concepts:** SELECT, WHERE, GROUP BY, aggregate functions, CASE WHEN, Window Functions and JOINS.
